# Security Policy

## Scope

Caribbean Asteroid Twin is a public, static, browser-side portfolio demonstrator. It has no accounts, database, backend, customer data, API keys, or external operational dataset.

Security reports are welcome for issues involving source code, dependencies, deployment behavior, unsafe links, browser execution, asset loading, or misleading security-sensitive behavior.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Do not publish exploit details, credentials, personal information, or sensitive reproduction steps in a public issue.

Use GitHub's private **Report a vulnerability** flow when available. If it is unavailable, open a minimal public issue requesting a private reporting channel without including sensitive details.

Please include:

- affected route, component, version, or commit;
- realistic impact and prerequisites;
- minimal reproduction steps;
- affected browsers or devices;
- suggested mitigation, when known.

## Testing boundaries

Do not perform denial-of-service testing, social engineering, scanning of third-party infrastructure, or testing against Vercel or other services without explicit authorization.

Reports that the scenario is fictional or not scientifically accurate are not security vulnerabilities; those are documented product boundaries.
